## Vyshanth's feedback

### 1. Results

Vyshanth said he was pleased with the progress. He noted that 92.5% on each of the held-out and test splits, and
95.5% on the 200-question evaluation, are above the agreed 90% accuracy target. He said the zero damaged valid
cells matter most to him, because they show the system gives useful answers while keeping the data intact. In his
view, accuracy, user approvals and a traceable change history together meet the practical goals behind DataSage.

### 2. Rule-breaking rows outside the question's columns

He asked us to keep checking only the columns relevant to the user's question in this final version. He said this
keeps the workflow focused and matches his earlier decision (October 28 review, row 9). He found the team's
explanation of the remaining misses made the evaluation easy to understand, and he supports presenting the recorded
results together with this clearly stated scope.

### 3. Hard-split grouping fix

He called the 80 of 80 result across the two hard-split seeds, with zero damaged cells, a strong outcome. He
appreciated that the grouping behaviour was fixed in the analysis code and then checked with fresh runs. He said
keeping the original and the post-fix results separate makes the improvement easy to follow, and that this is a
good example of tracing an observed behaviour to a specific, measured fix.

### 4. Ratings (A1)

He agreed that correctness, clarity and trust are the right criteria for rating the reports. He said he values
being able to see what changed, why it changed and whether it was approved, and that the cleaned copy and the
replay script make the output useful beyond the immediate answer.

Status when these notes were written: the ratings summary (`docs/final/ratings.md`) is not on `main` yet. Arsha
owns A1.

### 5. December 2 demo

He said the planned three-minute walkthrough suits the poster session. In his view, showing a sample file, a
plain-English question, an approval decision and the resulting answer would communicate the project's value
clearly. He said the Table, Answer and Data health views make the workflow easy to explain, and he supports
having a backup recording.

### Other comments

- He supports keeping the current interface and the finalized evaluation results.
- He said the 30B default fits his earlier preference for a responsive workflow.
- He sees preserving the original file and providing an auditable cleaned copy as real strengths.
- He sees clear progress from the original fixed analysis script to a question-driven cleaning and analysis
  workflow.

## Decisions

| # | Topic | Decision |

| 2 | Results | Keep the finalized evaluation results as recorded. |
